Summary

  • Healthcare, office/admin/tech, and food service are the top three industries students want to enter — 41% of all mapped career goals.
  • Biggest opportunity gap: healthcare and office/admin/tech. Far more students want these jobs than currently hold one; food service and cleaning show the opposite pattern.
  • Most students (122 of 168) said their primary education goal is simply to improve their English — career-specific goals are the minority.
  • See the FY27 Program Fit page for how these interests map against the specific training programs under consideration, including which ones are blocked or need more data.
